Overview

Four years of monthly gatherings, 8,150+ people, 29 events — and a platform designed to get lighter as the archive grows heavier.

29 events, 40 speakers, 60 startups — not isolated entries, but a connected system where relationships are as visible as the records.
A living archive — every gathering adds to a growing, searchable record that reflects the community's trajectory over time.
One entry, fully managed — a non-technical team runs the entire cadence. No duplication. No drift between what happens and what's published.

Challenge

A growing community, a growing maintenance burden

Each month brought a new event — along with speakers, startups, and flyers — but no structure connecting them. Content lived in isolated pieces, making it harder to manage, scale, and reflect the community as a whole.

Approach

Model the community, not the content

Instead of designing pages first, the system was built around relationships — events, speakers, and startups as connected collections. The website became a layer on top of structured data, not a container for disconnected content.

Outcome

An archive that grows itself

A single CMS entry per event populates the entire platform — from homepage sections to archive pages and profiles. As new events are added, the system expands automatically, without duplication or manual updates.

Design

Editorial, not ornamental

The design stays intentionally minimal — not to stand out, but to support clarity at scale. As the archive grows, typography and spacing carry the hierarchy, ensuring content remains readable and structured over time.

Rather than adding visual complexity, the focus is on consistency, predictability, and making relationships easy to scan across events, speakers, and startups.

CMS Architecture

Four collections.
One community.

Events are the central collection — every other collection connects to them. Speakers and Startups are independent profiles referenced by multiple events. Flyers form a separate image archive that auto-populates the archive page. No content is duplicated; everything is referenced.

Events

Central collection. Holds the date, flyer, registration link, and multi-reference fields to Speakers and Startups

Speakers

Referenced by Events. One speaker entry — name, photo, LinkedIn — appears across every event they participated in

Startups

Referenced by Events. Each startup entry links to the event where it pitched — building a searchable directory over time

Flyers

Separate image collection. Each flyer is its own entry — auto-populates the archive grid without any manual page updates

Relational schema

Add one event. The rest of the site updates itself.

Connected Experience

From event to everything else.
